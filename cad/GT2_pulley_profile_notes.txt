================================

Motor outline: cad/file_07_40.gif (Minebea PM42 file_07_40)

--------------

Total height = 1.38 mm
PLD          = 0.254 mm
Tip radius   = R0.555  (center on tooth CL)
Flank radius = R1.00   (labeled center offset 0.40 from CL)
Root fillet  = R0.15

Pulley (12T, this rebuild)
-------------------------
z            = 12
PD           = z*P/π = 12*2/π = 7.6394 mm
OD           = PD − 2×PLD = 7.6394 − 0.508 = 7.1314 mm
Length L     = 6.00 mm (no flanges)
Bore         = Ø3.00 mm
Clearance    = 0.05 mm (groove larger than belt tooth; radial)

How belt → pulley was inverted
------------------------------
1. Belt land (valley between teeth) rides at pulley OD.
2. Belt tooth tip seats in the pulley groove → groove depth ≈ tooth height
   + clearance (0.75 + 0.05 = 0.80 mm from OD toward axis).
3. Groove outline = belt tooth outline with radii enlarged by clearance:
     tip  R = 0.555 + 0.05 = 0.605 mm, center on CL at y = H_clr − R_tip
     root R = 0.15  + 0.05 = 0.20  mm, centers at x = ±(0.75 + 0.02) mm
4. Tip and root circles (with clearance) intersect; join at the upper
   intersection → closed cut polygon, circular-patterned ×12.
5. Front Plane sketch; extrude/cut along +Z.

Why R1.00 @ 0.40 was not used as a separate flank arc
------------------------------------------------------
A G1 (tangent) blend of tip R0.555 and flank R1.00 requires center-distance
|Rf − Rt| = 0.445 mm. That forces 2×Rt = 1.11 > Rf = 1.00, so the near-side
tooth join is geometrically impossible (contact falls on the far side of the
tip circle). Empirically, published GT2 polygon profiles (and this drawing’s
tip/root) are well-fit by tip R0.555 + root R0.15 alone; the labeled R1@0.40
is retained as drawing intent but omitted from the CAD flank as a separate
arc. Lateral X-scale of a forced R1@0.40 construction was rejected as it
distorts the tip radius.
